We run the six required methods under stratified 5-fold CV (seed~42).
Median fill, missing indicators, one-hot encoding and standardization are
fit on each fold's training rows only.
Labels are $\approx 8.9\%$ positive, so F1 uses a cutoff chosen on that fold's
validation scores (defaults: score~$\ge 0$, probability~$\ge 0.5$).
Ridge $\lambda\in\{10^{-4},10^{-3},10^{-2},10^{-1}\}$;
reg.\ logistic $\lambda\in\{10^{-4},10^{-3},10^{-2}\}$;
MSE-GD: $300$ steps, $\gamma{=}0.05$; logistic: $500$ steps, $\gamma{=}0.5$;
SGD: $10^{5}$ updates, $\gamma{=}0.01$.

Least squares, ridge ($\lambda{=}10^{-3}$) and MSE-GD are tied
(F1~$0.422$/$0.422$/$0.421$).
Logistic is best among the six (F1~$0.430\pm 0.004$; AUROC~$0.86$);
$\ell_2$ does not help further.
SGD yielded non-finite scores on every fold.
For submission we used logistic refit on all data, with cutoff~$0.196$.
