Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) accounted for approximately 19.8 million deaths in 2022 \cite{who2025}. Large-scale health surveys, such as the Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS), provide valuable data for studying factors associated with coronary heart disease. Missing values, redundant features, and class imbalance pose challenges,as positive cases may be missed despite high accuracy \cite{brfss2026}. The project investigates the classification of self-reported coronary heart disease using said BRFSS data, using linear models. The advantage of this being that the models are easy to interpret.
