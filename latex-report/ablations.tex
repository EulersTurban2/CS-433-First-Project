From ridge $\lambda{=}10^{-3}$ (F1~$0.422$), we change one choice and report
the mean fold-wise F1 difference $\Delta$ (Table~\ref{tab:ablations}).
The decision cutoff dominates the F1 score; undersampling $1{:}3$ and clinical products (age/BMI/health-day squares andrisk-factor products) give small gains; unpenalized intercept and
class-weighted logistic change almost nothing once the cutoff is retuned.

\begin{table}[h!]
  \centering
  \caption{Ablations vs.\ ridge $10^{-3}$ (class weight vs.\ unweighted logistic).}
  \label{tab:ablations}
  \setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
  \begin{tabular}{lcccc}
    \toprule
    Change & $\Delta$ F1 & F1 \\
    \midrule
    Default cutoff & $-0.320$ & $0.102$ \\
    Undersample $1{:}3$ / $1{:}1$ & $+0.006$ / $+0.004$ & $0.428$ / $0.426$ \\
    Clinical products & $+0.007$ & $0.429$ \\
    Unpen.\ intercept / class weight & $+0.000$ / $-0.001$ & $0.422$ / $0.429$ \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
